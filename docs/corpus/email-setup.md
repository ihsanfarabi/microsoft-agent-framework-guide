# Email Setup

Corporate email runs on MafMail and is configured automatically on company laptops and enrolled phones. Manual setup requires the server `mail.mafcorp.example` on port 993 with SSL.

Mailboxes are capped at 50 GB. You will receive a warning at 90% capacity; at 100% you cannot send or receive mail until space is freed.

Shared team mailboxes (for example `support@` and `invoices@`) are requested through the IT portal and require the owning manager's approval.

External forwarding rules are disabled by default. Exceptions require Security team approval and are logged.

The email retention policy keeps messages for 7 years. Deleted items are purged from the trash folder after 30 days.
