# License Management

All software licenses are purchased centrally by the Procurement team. Individual departments must not buy licenses directly from vendors.

Licenses are reconciled on the first business day of each month. Assignments unused for 90 days are reclaimed automatically.

Designated license owners can reassign a license to a different employee through the IT portal; reassignment takes effect within 1 business day.

Renewals are reviewed 60 days before expiry. Any license renewing above $10,000 per year requires a director's signature.

Trial conversions to paid licenses must be requested at least 10 business days before the trial ends to avoid a provisioning gap.
