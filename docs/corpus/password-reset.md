# Password Reset

Passwords expire every 90 days and must be at least 12 characters long. Reuse of any of your last 5 passwords is blocked.

To reset your password, go to id.mafcorp.example, click "Forgot password", and enter your corporate email. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 15 minutes.

If you are locked out after 5 failed attempts, the account freezes for 30 minutes. Locked-out users can call the help desk at extension 4357 for an assisted reset.

Managers cannot reset passwords on behalf of their team members. Every reset must be performed by the account owner or the help desk.

Password managers are encouraged; MafCorp provides a corporate license for PassVault to all employees.
