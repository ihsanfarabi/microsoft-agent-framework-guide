# WiFi Setup

Company WiFi is provided on three SSIDs: `MafCorp-Secure` for employee laptops, `MafCorp-Mobile` for phones and tablets, and `MafCorp-Guest` for visitors.

`MafCorp-Secure` uses WPA2-Enterprise. Connect with your corporate email and password; a certificate profile is pushed automatically by MDM after enrollment.

Guest WiFi passwords rotate weekly and are posted at reception. Guest WiFi is internet-only and has no access to internal systems.

Personal devices may join `MafCorp-Mobile` only after enrolling in MDM. Streaming and large downloads on guest WiFi are deprioritized during business hours.

For recurring WiFi dropouts, toggle the WiFi radio off and on, then re-authenticate to the SSID. Persistent issues should be reported with your machine's asset tag.
