# Backup Policy

Backups run nightly at 2am to the Franklin region. Each nightly backup is retained for 35 days.

Workstations are not backed up automatically; employees must store work files in the corporate drive, which is included in the nightly backup window.

Monthly archives are taken on the first Sunday of each month and retained for 7 years to meet audit requirements.

Restores are requested through the IT portal and typically complete within 4 hours for files and within 24 hours for full systems.

Backup success rates are reviewed weekly by the Infrastructure team. Three consecutive failed nights for any system trigger a SEV-2 incident.
