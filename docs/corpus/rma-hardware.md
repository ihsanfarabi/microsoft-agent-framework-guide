# RMA Hardware Returns

Faulty company hardware is replaced under warranty through the RMA process. RMA requests must be filed within 14 days of failure. Late requests require a director's approval.

To file an RMA, open a ticket in the IT portal under "Hardware > RMA", attach a photo of the fault, and the serial number of the device.

The IT team ships a replacement within 3 business days of an approved RMA. You must return the faulty unit within 10 business days of receiving the replacement, using the prepaid label provided.

Devices with physical damage or liquid exposure are not covered by RMA and are routed to the accident-damage process instead.

Loaner laptops are available for up to 15 business days while an RMA is in progress. Loaners must be returned the same day your replacement arrives.
