# Security Incident Response

Report suspected security incidents to security@mafcorp.example immediately, or call the 24/7 hotline at extension 7911. Do not attempt to investigate on your own.

Phishing emails should be reported using the "Report Phish" button in MafMail. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, disconnect from the network and call the hotline right away.

The Security team triages reports within 1 hour and classifies them as SEV-1 through SEV-4. SEV-1 incidents trigger the on-call pager and a status page post.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours so they can be remotely wiped. Device wipes erase all corporate data but leave personal data untouched on enrolled phones.

Post-incident reviews are held within 5 business days for any SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident.
