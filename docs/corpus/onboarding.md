# Employee Onboarding

New hires at MafCorp complete onboarding within their first 5 working days. On day one, IT issues a laptop, a badge, and a temporary password that must be changed at first login.

Employees get 25 vacation days per year. Up to 5 unused vacation days roll over into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited on January 31.

Laptops are refreshed every 4 years. Employees in Engineering and Design are eligible for refresh after 3 years if their current machine no longer meets tooling requirements.

Each new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team and must complete security-awareness training within 30 days of their start date.

Hardware requests, badge access, and software entitlements are all filed through the IT portal at it.mafcorp.example.
